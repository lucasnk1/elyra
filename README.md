## Vision

Elyra is a premium AI-powered technology intelligence platform designed to curate, organize, and surface the most important signals shaping the future of technology.

Built with a modern Silicon Valley-inspired product philosophy, Elyra combines intelligent news aggregation, AI summarization, semantic ranking, and a refined editorial experience into a single futuristic platform.

The goal is to transform overwhelming streams of tech information into a clean, intelligent, and beautifully curated experience.
